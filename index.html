Am I affected?

  1. Settings → Apps → Installed apps. Search LG Monitor, then McAfee. Either one you didn't install yourself means you were hit.
  2. Win+R → perfmon /rel. Look for LGElectronics.LGMonitorApp.
  3. Techs: pnputil /enum-drivers. LG entries mean the machine is still armed even if the app is gone.

Get it off

Two steps. Do only the first and the next LG panel brings it back.

1. The app

Get-AppxPackage -AllUsers LGElectronics.LGMonitorApp | Remove-AppxPackage -AllUsers

2. Both delivery packages

pnputil /enum-drivers
pnputil /delete-driver oemXX.inf /uninstall

Find the two LG Electronics entries — lgmonitorappextension.inf and lgmonitorappsoftwarecomponent.inf — and delete each by the oemXX.inf name Windows gave it. Windows Home, and the caveats → · On a TV →
