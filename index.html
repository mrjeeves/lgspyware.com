Consumer alert · LG

One screen lets software in. The other sends the room out.

Connect an affected LG monitor to a Windows PC and LG's app is installed 32 seconds later, by SYSTEM, with nothing asked. Unplug the monitor and it stays. Its televisions are worse.

Am I affected? Remove it
By Critical Error Computing · Updated September 12, 2026
  • 32 seconds. Windows Update hands over two LG packages signed as drivers. Neither contains driver code. One of them tells Windows to fetch LG's app from the Store, and SYSTEM installs it. No window, no EULA, nothing in Programs and Features.
  • It outlives the monitor. Ours was unplugged in October 2025. Windows was still updating the app nine months later.
  • Removing the app isn't enough. Both packages stay in the driver store, wired to 51 hardware IDs. Plug in any of those and it comes back.
  • The televisions map the house. Devices never paired with the set, neighbouring Wi-Fi names, transcripts from audio with the screen off, and roughly 4GB of viewing data a month. On HDMI alone one set sent 20 packets a minute to LG's ad arm, carrying a location good to the nearest major road.
  • LG's answer: “The claims made in the recently published video are not true.”

Am I affected?

If an affected LG monitor has ever touched this PC, check. Unplugging it doesn't undo anything.

  1. Settings → Apps → Installed apps. Search LG Monitor, then McAfee. Either one you didn't install yourself means you were hit.
  2. Win+R → perfmon /rel. Look for LGElectronics.LGMonitorApp. Windows logs it as a successful update rather than an app install.
  3. For techs: pnputil /enum-drivers. LG Electronics entries mean the machine is still armed even if the app is gone.

Get it off

Two steps. Do only the first and the next LG panel you plug in brings it straight back.

1. The app

Get-AppxPackage -AllUsers LGElectronics.LGMonitorApp | Remove-AppxPackage -AllUsers

2. Both delivery packages

pnputil /enum-drivers
pnputil /delete-driver oemXX.inf /uninstall

Find the two entries from LG Electronics Inc. in that list — lgmonitorappextension.inf and lgmonitorappsoftwarecomponent.inf — and delete each by the oemXX.inf name Windows gave it. Everything else on the machine keeps its drivers. Windows Home, and the caveats →

On a TV: Settings → General → System → Additional Settings, then turn off Live Plus and limit ad tracking. The rest →
