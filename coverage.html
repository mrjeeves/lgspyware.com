03 · Open

What we're watching for

A second labNobody outside the original team has reproduced or refuted the standby audio finding, which is the claim LG denies most directly.
A CVEThe webOS code-execution bugs are still under responsible disclosure. No identifier and no firmware fix have been published.
A build that asksLG said on August 3 that its monitor installer would start asking before it installs. Nothing has shipped since August 12.
A regulatorTexas settled with LG over viewing data in May. Whether anyone reopens that after September is an open question.
