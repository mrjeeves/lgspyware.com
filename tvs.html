01 · The findings

What a television did on a bench

Two researchers, MrBruh and uturn, worked with Steve Burke of Gamers Nexus and Wendell Wilson of Level1Techs. Retail sets, packet captures, standby measured rather than assumed.

It maps your home

Devices on the local network that were never paired with the TV: phones, watches, routers, thermostats, air purifiers, server management controllers, PCs.

It maps your street

Names, signal strengths and channels of neighbouring Wi-Fi networks, alongside the household's public IP and internal addresses.

It listens in standby

Plaintext transcripts generated from audio captured while the screen is off — the finding LG disputes most directly.

Offline is a delay, not a stop

Disconnected from the internet, a set kept capturing and stored locally, then transmitted once connectivity returned.

It watches every input

Content recognition samples audio and video across inputs — including whatever you have plugged into HDMI.

Roughly 4GB a month

Viewing-recognition data from a single test set, routed to LG's advertising infrastructure.

02 · The response

What LG denies, and what it confirms

LG answered the press rather than the researchers. Its denial is narrow and precise — worth reading against what was actually alleged.

LG says

“LG TVs process voice data only when the voice button on the remote control is pressed and held, or when a wake word such as ‘Hi LG’ is recognized after the user has activated the Far-Field voice recognition feature.” Other than these instances, “the TVs do not collect or record ambient conversations.”

The allegation it meets

A direct contradiction on the central point, and the one a second lab settles rather than a statement. What the researchers describe is not ambient conversation but transcripts produced with the screen off. Nobody outside LG has reproduced or refuted it yet.

LG says

Local network scanning is standard functionality for device connectivity, common to smart TVs and media players.

The allegation it meets

Confirmed, not denied. The dispute is not whether the TV enumerates your thermostat and your neighbour's Wi-Fi, but whether a television needs to.

LG says

Content recognition runs on user consent, and unrecognised audio is processed locally and deleted immediately.

The allegation it meets

Consent is exactly what Texas made LG rewrite in May. The claim is about the setup screen; the finding is about volume — roughly 4GB a month from one set.

Statements as given to The Register and Tom's Hardware, Sep 8–11
03 · The security half

The part that isn't about advertising

Alongside the data collection, the researchers reported remote code execution flaws in webOS: a chain that starts with the television's own browser rendering a malicious page and ends with a forged “a mobile device would like to pair with your TV” prompt. A compromised set sits inside the home network with a microphone attached.

Unresolved as of September 12, 2026

Technical details are withheld while responsible disclosure runs. No CVE has been published and no firmware fix has shipped, and LG has not addressed the vulnerabilities publicly.

This is the one finding where waiting is the correct behaviour from everyone involved — including us. It is listed here so it isn't lost in the advertising story, not because there is anything to act on yet.

Malwarebytes, Sep 7
04 · The record

Three years, three findings, one answer

2013An LG TV sent the filenames on a user's USB drive back to LG, and kept transmitting after he opted out. LG admitted it.
Dec 2025Texas sues five TV makers over content recognition. LG's US unit settles on May 11, 2026 — consent before collection, a disclosure screen, a real opt-out, no transfers to the CCP. No admission of liability.
Jul 21, 2026Spur finds residential-proxy code in more than 42% of apps on LG's webOS store. LG says non-compliant apps will be suspended.
Sep 6, 2026The investigation above — four months after the Texas settlement rewrote LG's consent flow.

The throughline is not a single product. It is that the decision to collect is made once, centrally, and the consent screen is edited afterwards.

The same company, on your desk

Plug in an affected LG monitor and Windows installs LG's app in 32 seconds, with no prompt. That is what this site started on, and it is still unfixed.

05 · Tonight

What you can turn off

None of this removes the network scanning, and none of it touches the unpatched bug. It does cut what leaves the set.

  1. Content recognition. Settings → All Settings → General → System → Additional Settings → turn off Live Plus. This is the ACR switch.
  2. Ad personalisation. Same menu → Advertisement → Limit Ad Tracking. Reset the advertising ID while you're there.
  3. Voice. Turn off Far-Field voice recognition if you don't use it — by LG's own account, that is the feature that opens the microphone without the remote.
  4. The blunt instrument. Take the TV off Wi-Fi and feed it from a streaming stick or a PC. The set keeps capturing; it loses the route home.

Menu names move between webOS versions and regions. If yours differ, the wording to look for is Live Plus, Additional Settings and Advertisement.
